    How this was built

    Who counts as elite

    A player is in the pool if he was among the 50 highest-rated players of a season in the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 or the Champions League in at least three of the ten seasons, or won a major individual honour such as a Ballon d'Or top-30 finish, Golden Boy or Kopa Trophy. Players born in 2002 or later need two seasons. The pool is not ranked and no score is shown, because a rating-based order favours long careers in these five leagues.

    Where a player was trained

    The country of the youth club he was at by age 15, from the youth-career lists on Wikipedia. England, Scotland and Wales are separated by club. First youth team and final nation come from Wikidata.

    Club locations

    Each pin is the club's home stadium from Wikidata. Where a club has no stadium listed, its own coordinates or headquarters city are used.

    Limits

    Counts are small for most nations, so close gaps are within noise. Youth career lists on Wikipedia are incomplete, goalkeepers and defenders are underrepresented in ratings, and academy regions inside a country are not covered.
