Germany’s talent factories: which clubs and regions actually build the players who reach Europe’s top five leagues?

    How this was built

    Who is in the data

    Every player born in Germany between 1965 and 2005 who made at least 20 appearances for a club in the Bundesliga, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A or Ligue 1, which covers playing careers from 1990 to 2026. Born in Germany is the only nationality test. That gives 1,047 players: 494 born 1985 or later and 553 born 1965 to 1984. 520 have played for Germany; the rest play for other nations or were never capped by Germany. Players who trained abroad (Nmecha, Schlupp and Soares among them) are left out, because the question is who learned the game in Germany. No rating or score is used.

    First appearance year

    The slider adds players by the year of their first appearance in one of the five leagues, from 1990 to 2026, and the map builds up as it moves. From 2013 on this is the date of the first league match in the match data. Before that, and for older careers, it is the first season at a club in one of the five leagues, taken from Wikipedia career tables, so it can be a year early for a player who signed before he played. The 1990 position includes the players who had already made their first appearance by then.

    Two sources, two ways of counting

    For players born 1985 or later, appearances come from league match data (July 2012 to June 2026) and count only Big-5 league games. For players born 1965 to 1984, appearances come from the career tables on Wikipedia and count every game for a club that plays in one of the five leagues, including second-division seasons. The two groups are therefore close but not identical, and the older group's counts run a little high.

    Which youth club counts

    Last youth club is the German professional club whose youth side the player was in just before turning professional, one credit per player. First pro academy is the first professional club in his youth career. Any academy counts every professional club he passed through. Amateur clubs are not counted. 303 players have no professional academy on record. Most came up through amateur clubs and signed straight for a pro club, and for older players Wikipedia youth lists are often incomplete. They are credited to the pro club they first signed for and marked "Entered pro football at" on the player card.

    Where the youth paths come from

    Youth transfers from the Transfermarkt dataset published by dcaribou (born 1985 or later), combined with English Wikipedia youth careers (all players). Where both exist they name the same last youth club in 92 of 93 cases, and Transfermarkt's own club hierarchy confirms the club matching for all 98 players checked.

    Inside and outside the Bundesliga

    Counted from league appearances: Bundesliga against the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1. Other leagues are not in the data. "Other Big-5 league" means at least five appearances in one of those four.

    Limits

    Youth lists are incomplete for some players, especially older ones. Close gaps between clubs are within noise. Differences of one or two players between clubs are within noise. Club pins are home stadiums, not academy grounds.
