    How this was built

    Who is in the data

    Every player born in Germany (Transfermarkt birth country), born 1985 or later, with at least 20 league appearances in the Bundesliga, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A or Ligue 1 between July 2012 and June 2026. That is 513 players. Thirteen are left out because they trained abroad (Nmecha, Schlupp and Soares among them) or no German club could be traced, which leaves 500. 414 have played for Germany; the rest play for Turkey, Poland, Croatia, the USA and other nations, or were never capped. Birth in Germany is the only entry test, and no rating or score is used.

    Why not more than 500

    Germany internationals since 1990 number about 264. Players born in Germany with real Big-5 careers are a larger group, and 500 is what a 20-appearance cut gives. At 10 appearances it is about 600, at 1 appearance over 800. Players born before 1985 are outside the Transfermarkt coverage and are not included.

    Which youth club counts

    Last youth club is the German professional club whose youth side the player was in just before turning professional, one credit per player. First pro academy is the first professional club in his youth career. Any academy counts every professional club he passed through. Amateur clubs are not counted. 40 players came up through amateur clubs and signed straight for a pro club; they are credited to that club and marked "Entered pro football at" on the player card.

    Where the youth paths come from

    Youth transfers from the Transfermarkt dataset published by dcaribou, combined with English Wikipedia youth careers. Where both exist they name the same last youth club in 92 of 93 cases, and Transfermarkt's own club hierarchy confirms the club matching for all 98 players checked.

    Inside and outside the Bundesliga

    Counted from league appearances: Bundesliga against the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1. Other leagues are not in the data. "Other Big-5 league" means at least five appearances in one of those four.

    Limits

    Appearance data starts July 2012, so older careers are cut short. Youth lists are incomplete for some players. Differences of one or two players between clubs are within noise. Club pins are home stadiums, not academy grounds.
